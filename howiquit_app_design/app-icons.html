What to export

02

App Store

1024 x 1024 PNG, no alpha channel, no rounded corners, no transparency. The system applies its own mask, which is why the OS mask overlay is drawn on every tile above.

Play Store

512 x 512 PNG, 32-bit, no alpha. The same square art works as-is; export it by scaling the 1024 SVG down.

Android adaptive

Foreground layer with the mark inside the dashed 66% circle, background layer as flat black. Continuum bleeds a line off the right edge on purpose, so give it the full-bleed foreground and let the line crop.

Colour

Spectral white on pure black only. Two values, one mark, no gradient and no colour, in line with the rest of the product.

Fonts

Zero and QS are set in type. Convert both to outlines before exporting so the shipped icon does not depend on a font being installed.
