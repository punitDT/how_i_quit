System surfaces · iOS + Android

Where the timer lives
outside the app

The Smoke-Free Timer runs whether or not the app is open, so it has to be legible on the lock screen, in the Dynamic Island, on the home screen and in a notification. Every surface below carries the same number, the same rule and the same one filled action.

Light and dark invert together. Lock-screen, StandBy and Dynamic Island stay dark in both themes because the OS keeps them dark over the wallpaper — the inversion never applies to a photo or the Island.

Notifications

04

Two kinds only: a target-unlocked moment, and a zone nudge. Both are non-judgemental, both are actionable, and a slip never produces a notification — the timer simply keeps running.

Target unlocked10:00
Quit Smokenow Nine hours done. The timer runs to twelve next. OpenNot now

Sent when the day's target completes, never before. The copy states the next number instead of congratulating.

Trigger zone11:40

A zone nudge offers the routine, not a warning. "Walk" jumps straight into Craving Escape, step one.
