Quit Smoke

You want to quit. Cravings make it hard.

Sound familiar? Quitting is not really about willpower. It is about what you do in the few minutes after a craving shows up. You can plan that part.

The craving

A craving feels endless. It isn't.

Most cravings fade in a few minutes. You do not need more willpower. You need something to do with your body until the craving is gone.

PeakWhere the urge is strongest
FadeWhere it lets go by itself

Tool one · Craving Escape

Sitting still with the urge is what fails.

A craving is at its worst when you just sit with it. Move instead. Start moving while it is at its peak, and keep going until it fades.

Walk

Get up and move while it is at its peak.

Breathe

Slow breaths while it is fading.

Move on

It clears. You did not smoke.

Optional. Craving Escape walks you through the peak and logs the moment as smoke-free.

Tool two · Smoke-Free Timer

The timer grows. Then it runs without stopping.

Timer running → I don't smoke.

Each day the timer holds for three hours longer than the day before. After Day 7 it keeps running for the length of your plan. You choose the hours it runs, so it covers the times you usually smoke.

Your slots

Pick the hours you smoke most, or the hours cravings hit hardest. The timer runs in the slots you choose and stays quiet outside them.

3 slots picked · about 7 hours a day covered
